Vegetable Pasta

A quick pasta recipe made with fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mixed vegetables
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta.
  2. Heat the sauce and vegetables.
  3. Mix everything together.
  4. Serve warm.

Fruit Salad

A fresh and healthy fruit salad for any time of day.

Ingredients

  • Apple
  • Banana
  • Orange
  • Grapes

Instructions

  1. Wash the fruits.
  2. Cut them into small pieces.
  3. Mix them in a bowl.
  4. Serve immediately.
